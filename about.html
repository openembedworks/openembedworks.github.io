Our Mission

OpenEmbedWorks aims to democratize access to engineering tools by building a curated ecosystem of browser-native utilities that run client-side, require zero installation, and respect user privacy.

We believe that great engineering tools should be:

  • Accessible – No barriers to entry. Click a link, start working.
  • Private – All computation happens in your browser. No data sent to servers.
  • Open – Source available for inspection, modification, and contribution.
  • Collaborative – Built by the community, for the community.
  • Offline-first – Works online and offline. Your choice.

What Makes Us Different

🌐 Browser-Native, Not Native Apps

We believe the browser has evolved into a powerful platform. Our tools run entirely in the browser—no installers, no system dependencies, no bloat. One URL, instant access.

🔐 Privacy-First Design

All computation is client-side. You own your data. No telemetry, no analytics, no third-party tracking. What happens in your browser stays in your browser.

📦 Curated, Not Overwhelming

We've carefully selected tools that solve real problems for embedded, systems, and automotive engineers. Quality over quantity. Each tool is vetted and tagged for easy discovery.

🔧 Offline-First

Once loaded, the entire catalog works offline. Service Workers ensure tools are available even without an internet connection. Perfect for teams in restricted or intermittent network environments.

💻 By Engineers, For Engineers

OpenEmbedWorks is built by working engineers who understand the challenges of systems and embedded development. We design for real workflows, not marketing narratives.
